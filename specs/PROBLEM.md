Problem

- As a person i want to make a trip with my friends, i want to know what is affordable for me/friends/family
  Solution
- Have a tailored made trip based our financial possibilities and habits
  UX
- User wants to do a trip with a group of people
- He gathers the people and set a common destination trip
- Checks the destination suggested by the app based on the behavior and financial availabilities of the group
- Once decided the destination they can set a common vault to start saving money for the trip
  Implementation
- Bank has created a financial profile per user based on his financials
- Bank also creates a preferences profile based on the transactional behavior
- Based on the above input we get insights on possible group travel destinations and experience
- The bank creates a common vault and after that it makes implementations - suggestions for the user.
  Bank Impact
- Bank can bring new users of the friends friends
- Bank will earn money on the transactions by saving money.
- Bank can have hotels or possible small travel destination to subscribe to the bank with a fee in order to be suggested as a travel destination.
